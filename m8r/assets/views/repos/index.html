Repos

The repos that m8r works in. A repo is a clone in the home of m8r's user, at its path, and the project on its forge, at its URL. A schedule runs in repos, and a watchlist item is in the repo whose project its own URL names.

Clone a repo as the m8r user, and then add it here by its path. Leave the URL blank, and m8r reads it from the clone's origin. A repo with no URL takes no watchlist items.
